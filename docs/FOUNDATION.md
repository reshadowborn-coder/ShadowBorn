# Shadowborn V1 Foundation

## Product rule

Gameplay presentation should be benchmarked against premium mobile hero RPGs for clarity, pacing, camera discipline, readable silhouettes and response. Shadowborn does not copy another game's copyrighted art, models, characters, maps, UI art or narrative.

## Non-negotiables

1. No directional movement controls in the core game loop.
2. World traversal is node based.
3. Battle rooms are authored camera compositions, not explorable levels.
4. The camera prioritizes faces, silhouettes, weapons and impact readability.
5. Every combatant has idle, windup, impact, recovery, hit and death presentation hooks.
6. Turn order is speed + turn meter.
7. Manual and AUTO use the same deterministic combat rules.
8. Skills are data driven and support cooldowns/statuses/turn-meter operations.
9. User-facing production scenes may not ship with primitive fallback characters.
10. iPhone 13 Pro / 60 FPS is the reference device budget.

## First art milestone

The first production-quality milestone is one sewer battle room with:
- one final Shadow skinned model;
- one final rat model and venom variant;
- authored idle/basic/A2/hit/death animation clips;
- final PBR materials;
- final lighting;
- final VFX and audio;
- finalized battle HUD.

Nothing after that milestone should expand campaign scope until this room passes the visual bar.
