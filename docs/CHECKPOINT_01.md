# Checkpoint 01 acceptance gate

Scope is frozen to awakening + first battle.

## Awakening
- close camera starts on the Shadow;
- fade from black;
- readable cold/warm lighting contrast;
- ruined crypt/cemetery composition;
- round ruined window landmark;
- slab and environmental storytelling;
- subtle suspended motes;
- Shadow rises before control is handed to combat;
- cinematic bars and minimal subtitles;
- sequence can be skipped.

## First battle
- one opponent: Grave Hound;
- no movement joystick;
- fixed authored camera;
- Shadow and enemy large enough to read;
- A1 and A2 only;
- speed/turn meter;
- manual, AUTO, x1/x2;
- anticipation → travel → impact → reaction → recovery;
- camera push on A2;
- damage text and impact flash;
- death presentation;
- test checkpoint ends immediately after victory/defeat.

## Do not expand
No Temple hub, campaign map, forge, merchant, second encounter or sewer act until the user tests this checkpoint.
